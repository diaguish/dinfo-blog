## Why Dinfo ?

I've always been curious about how technology works, evolves, and reshapes the world around us. After a degree in software engineering and now diving deep into AI & Data, I realized I wanted a space to share what I learn — not just store it in a notebook somewhere.

**Dinfo** is that space. The name is a double meaning: *The info*, and *D* for Diago.

## What you'll find here

- **AI & Machine Learning** — research, tools, experiments
- **Cybersecurity** — news, concepts, good practices  
- **Dev** — tips, discoveries, languages
- **Tech news** — what matters and why

## Who am I ?

I'm Diago — software engineering grad, now specializing in AI & Data. I believe technology is reshaping every corner of our world, and I think it matters to understand it, question it, and talk about it clearly.

This blog is my way of doing that.

*Stay curious. — D*
